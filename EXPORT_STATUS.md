# Hide iT 0.0.10 · Windows playtest

The title screen offers two offline missions, **Asylum / Night Shift** and
**Garage / Lower Level**. Pick a character and a map, then select **Start Mission**.
The pause and result screens can take you back to the title to switch maps.
The main and pause menus include **Controls**. You can change your character
from the pause menu without restarting the mission.

Carry the body from intake to the service dumpster. Press **E** when **Stash body**
appears. The mission completes after the lid closes. Use **R**, **Enter**, or
**Restart** to try again.

Patrol cones show where guards can see. A guard who sees you gives chase and must
catch up to stop your run. Hide in a closet or under a marked bed after breaking
line of sight. Guards can catch you if they see you enter. Press **G** to throw a
rock and draw nearby guards to the impact, including guards inspecting the body.
In the garage, use parked cars and pillars to break sight. The east passage goes
through maintenance, while the west drive leads directly to the loading bay.

The Windows archive includes the playable build. The source checkout requires the
local character and environment packs listed in [README](README.md). The Garage
scenery uses parts of the [PSX City sample](https://zenthrall.itch.io/ultimate-psx-city-pack-free-sample)
and [PSX Submarine sample](https://zenthrall.itch.io/psx-submarine-kit-free-sample)
by Zenthrall Games.

Launch with `-hideit-lab` to open the original physics and animation training room.
