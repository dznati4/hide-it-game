Their strongest common evidence is visual: readable clothing blocks, expressive human shapes, organized environment masses and visible interaction. Their technical honesty is a strength: none claims to have established the references' triangle counts, skeletons, shaders, marketplace provenance or networking internals. This director has reviewed those accounts, not independently replayed every referenced trailer. Preserve that attribution when summarizing them.

Independent source spot-check: opened all six official Steam pages and confirmed the intended game/developer identities: [Oar'some / Haymaker](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4555210/), [Headliners / KAFI](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3059070/The_Headliners/?l=english), [ROB RUN / Kefir](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), [skate. / Full Circle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3354750/), [RV There Yet? / Nuggets](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3949040/RV_There_Yet/), and [GTA V Legacy / Rockstar North](https://store.steampowered.com/app/271590/Grand_Theft_Auto_V_Legacy/). This verifies reference identity and developer attribution, not each visual claim or proprietary technique.

Reported screenshot observations are narrower evidence than hands-on play. Shared physical trouble is a useful design inference; it does not establish a reference's constraint solver or prove that the same idea will be funny in Hide iT. Developer marketing descriptions establish advertised features, not measured reliability or performance. Repeated visual elements suggest reuse, not proven modular asset construction. GTA evidence is correctly bounded to Legacy promotional stills; older gameplay evidence must not be presented as PC-specific runtime validation.

Resolve these differences explicitly:

- Reports 01–03 propose multiple material categories; reports 07–08 and the current implementation favor one palette atlas/material per character. **Use the shared 128×128 atlas for this study.** Cloth/skin/rubber remain visual categories, not mandatory material slots. Do not claim atlas use batches all skinned characters into one draw.
- Height proposals range from 1.75 to 1.85 m. Choose a documented anatomy target, then measure each exported mesh including headwear separately. A declared meter scene and a bone endpoint are not measured character height.
- Broad cartoon shapes and more grounded clothing are compatible; miniature details and photoreal surfaces are unnecessary. Approve one coherent hotel cast, not six competing reference styles.
- Report 04 proposes animation-to-ragdoll blending. That is a future architecture option, not behavior implemented by a static FBX or required by the current drag lab. Preserve the present mechanics until a deliberate incremental test exists.
- Online selection, guard detection, arrest/rescue, trolley and hiding-container UI are **concepts**. Label sample room codes, statuses and buttons as examples. The working offline Carry Lab must remain clearly accessible. A pose showing multiple carriers is not evidence of networked carrying.
- The no-blood requirement does not prohibit a red warning color. Warning meaning must also use icon/text. Blue body clothing is a role-study color, not a rule that future limp characters must change skin or clothing.

## P0 — blockers before playable art approval

1. **Deformation and weighting:** joined primitives are not a continuous anatomical mesh. Connected tapered rings improve each limb, but separate torso/limb surfaces can still intersect at shoulders and hips. Inspect the actual topology and posed result. Audit every exported vertex: at least one positive influence; finite, nonnegative weights; normalized sums within a stated tolerance; referenced groups map to actual exported bones; maximum influence count compatible with Unity import/runtime settings. Rigid headwear/shoes may legitimately have one influence. Record the audit counts, not only “rigged = true”. Exercise elbow/knee flexion near 90°, shoulder lift, hip bend, crouch and dragged poses; reject holes or severe collapsing at gameplay distance.
2. **Scale, orientation and bind pose:** import a one-meter reference and measure actual Unity bounds, sole-to-head height, widest costume extent and relevant doorway clearance. Require expected unit transforms, left/right identity, positive-Z facing in Unity and a consistent root/pivot. Log all 22 intended bones and bind poses. Generic is sufficient for physics-driven display. If Humanoid is claimed, inspect Unity mapping and T-pose; an A-pose or green auto-map alone is insufficient. Unity explicitly requires checking the avatar after mapping. [Unity avatar guidance](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/ConfiguringtheAvatar.html)
3. **Physics/render integration:** the 22-bone authoring rig and current 11-body ragdoll are different contracts. A parent transform alone cannot articulate the skin correctly. Either keep this work in an isolated art gallery or supply an explicit mapping with calibrated bind offsets and deliberately derived extra spine/neck/shoulder/hand/foot transforms. Ensure an Animator and physics driver do not compete. Do not change tested collider dimensions, joint settings, mass or grip locations as an incidental art replacement.
4. **Import and visibility:** verify each imported asset has the intended skinned renderer, material/submesh count, texture assignment, normals and bone weights; no missing/pink shader. Record triangle and vertex counts from evaluated/exported/imported geometry, because source polygon counts alone can miss conversion/modifier results. Test fully extended and offscreen/re-entering ragdolls. Imported animation bounds may not cover runtime physics deformation; choose and verify conservative bounds or an appropriate offscreen-update strategy. [Unity skinned renderer reference](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/class-SkinnedMeshRenderer.html)
5. **Readability:** inspect front/side/back plus actual third-person distance, then grayscale and small stream-view thumbnails. Security needs a cap/shoulder/belt outline; guest needs a softer hem/bag outline; player needs a distinctive jacket/cap outline. Body state needs limp posing, visible extremities and closed eyes, beyond color. Check navy against parking shadows and blue sleeves against slate floors. Hands/ankles and the next doorway must remain visible in both cameras. A flattering studio lineup cannot establish gameplay readability.
6. **Regression and truthful status:** if Carry Lab receives these visuals, rerun its six physics tests and rendered delivery/camera/reset acceptance on the changed build. Confirm the shown asset is the exported revision being tested. Keep renderer-only/gallery validation separate from live carry validation. Do not call new art “playable,” “network-ready,” “production-ready” or “approved” based only on file existence, import success or a studio render.

Unity supports configurable skin influences; four is a practical cap for this inexpensive study, not an engine-wide limit. Check the importer and renderer quality together. The official reference also distinguishes root-bone bounds and offscreen behavior. [Unity skin/render properties](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/class-SkinnedMeshRenderer.html)

## P1 — smallest convincing art milestone

Deliver one original adult base family, several ordinary selectable player outfits, distinct hotel security and civilian roles, and a blood-free limp-body study. The initial orange/navy/plum/blue lineup is a palette experiment, not the only permitted wardrobe. Player choices should vary outer contour (shirt, jacket, hoodie), hair, trousers and shoes, rather than imply different classes or abilities. Retain the 22-bone inventory unless an observed failure justifies more. Use one shared palette texture and one material per skin initially. The art director's provisional **4,000–7,000 triangles** per nearby character is a planning range, not a requirement to add invisible polygons. Report actual counts and silhouette/deformation quality first. Likewise, low prop/module budgets are targets, not measurements of any reference.

Build one hotel corridor corner and one parking corner around the **existing measured opening**, with wall/floor/corner/doorframe, a column, bay markings and a simple blocker. Keep decorative trim outside clearance and separate visual meshes from physics. A 0.5 m snapping grid is sensible; it does not justify rounding away the tested door dimensions. Put labels and contrast at handles/openings before adding ornamental detail. One warm public-space light treatment and cooler service/parking fill are enough. Validate in Unity's actual renderer; Blender lighting and color management are presentation choices.

Provide a compact review plate with role lineup, unlit silhouette/value checks, a bent-joint sample, and one imported Unity view. Include exact tool versions, generation time/revision, artifact names and measured manifest values. This supplies reviewable evidence while keeping scope realistic at zero asset cost. Do not expand into a large hotel, city population, wardrobe system or shader project before these gates pass.

## P2 — later polish

The new realism request moves adult anatomy, coherent face planes and believable clothing construction into the next art pass. Establish sloping shoulders, a neck-to-jaw transition, ribcage/pelvis taper and directional heel/toe shapes before more geometry. Shape a chin/jaw wedge and cheek planes, integrate the nose bridge, recess eyes under eyelids/brow ridge, and replace floating eye spheres/brow bars and flat hair caps. Garments need a clear front opening/overlap, collar, cuffs, flush pockets and two or three broad folds at elbows/hips/armpits; raised rectangular chest panels currently resemble armor. These changes matter more than pores, stitching or photoreal textures. A single matte atlas can support the form review; more realistic rendering later requires convincing light, contact shadows and restrained material-response differences, verified in Unity.

Later polish includes a second guest silhouette, three player identity accents, a small signage family and additional broad garment folds. Postpone facial rigs, finger physics, simulated cloth/hair, large wardrobes, cinematic animation, LOD production and elaborate environment dressing. Networking remains its own verified mechanics milestone, not an art-delivery claim.

## Added head-grab acceptance

The head grab is now requested working behavior, rather than an art concept. Make it the fifth named grip and keep visible targeting near the head/neck. Verify unobstructed selection, rejection behind solid walls, held-label accuracy, raise/drop/release, reset while holding and passage through the current test doorway. Test that a nearby torso or hand does not incorrectly win selection and that head/neck constraints remain stable under the maximum permitted force. Reuse the existing head rigidbody/collider first; a new grip does not require extra simulated anatomy. Update control/help text and five-grip evidence consistently. If this is tested on the primitive body while the new skin stays isolated in the gallery, say so explicitly.
