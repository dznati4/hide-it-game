# Grand Theft Auto V Legacy — bounded visual research for Hide iT

Research date: 22 November 2026. Target: an original, comic, 1–3-player hotel/parking stealth and body-carry prototype in Unity 6 and Blender 5.3, with no blood and no asset budget.

## Edition and evidence boundary

**Official statement:** [Steam app 271590](https://store.steampowered.com/app/271590/Grand_Theft_Auto_V_Legacy/) explicitly identifies **Grand Theft Auto V Legacy**, Rockstar North, Rockstar Games, and the 14 April 2015 PC release. Rockstar’s [4 March 2025 upgrade announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7551a7k917554a/free-upgrade-for-grand-theft-auto-v-on-pc-now-available) calls the original PC version Legacy and distinguishes it from Enhanced. Consequently, this study uses the **2015 PC image galleries**, not Enhanced ray tracing, later console comparisons, modded police footage, or current mixed-edition community captures.

**Direct visual inspection:** Rockstar’s [27 February 2015 PC screens](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/25o2411812ak13/new-grand-theft-auto-v-pc-screens) and [27 March 2015 PC screens](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/1748koo9o829a9/screens-from-grand-theft-auto-v-for-pc) were opened and inspected in-browser. They are selected promotional compositions, generally without gameplay HUD, rather than an exhaustive record of normal play. Their imagery supports appearance observations; it does not reveal implementation.

## Character and authority readability

**Visible facts:** The inspected PC stills show strong civilian clothing blocks: a dark blue outer shirt over a lighter hem, a backward cap and large shoulder bag; a checked shirt with rolled sleeves and jeans; a sleeveless light top against darker trousers; and a dark varsity jacket with yellow sleeves. Clothing length, exposed arms, accessories and colour placement differentiate figures before facial detail matters. The varsity jacket remains legible against dark industrial framing because its sleeves form two bright side masses.

**Limit:** These galleries do not provide a controlled, full-body ordinary-patrol-officer/civilian comparison. They do show pursuing police vehicles distinguished by emergency lights, contrasted with civilian cars. Do not turn that into an invented catalogue of Legacy officer uniforms, badges, equipment or body meshes.

**Hide iT inference/proposal:** Give the hotel guard a square shoulder line, short uniform jacket, cap and conspicuous radio silhouette. Give guests softer, varied hems and one large accessory each. Use a shared original base body if practical, but change outer contours, stance and value grouping. Test recognition as black silhouettes and at actual gameplay distance; tiny insignia should be supplementary. This is a proposed design solution, not a claim about Rockstar’s production pipeline.

## City, parking and material hierarchy

**Visible facts:** A dusk intersection still separates dark road, pale lane markings, raised kerbs, pedestrian edges, traffic-light poles and large building masses. Parked/moving car shapes create intermediate-scale landmarks. Another PC still places a convertible beside a worn grocery/liquor building: the sign, canopy, façade and ground plane establish location before small weeds and cables become noticeable. An industrial character image uses platforms, rails, stairs and vertical supports to communicate layered space.

Car panels have coherent, relatively smooth highlights; road and soil read as rougher, broken surfaces. Bright headlights and emergency lights punctuate darker compositions. These are visible highlight relationships, **not** evidence for particular shader equations, texture resolutions, metallic values or UV layouts.

**Hide iT proposal:** Build one parking court with a clear driveway, pedestrian strip, hotel entrance and service route. Use three large landmarks: coloured entrance canopy, service door and parking sign. Keep carry paths wider than the combined player/body silhouette. Model a small reusable family of pillars, kerbs, doors and parked-car blockers. Establish painted metal, concrete, cloth and glass with broad roughness/value differences before adding decals. Reserve the sharpest colour contrast for exits, usable containers and guard cues. Avoid copying Los Santos signs, street layouts or vehicles.

## Camera, HUD and physical reactions

**Official statement:** The Legacy Steam description confirms first-person mode and configurable PC presentation; its third-person tags are user-defined, not technical documentation. Rockstar’s [official gameplay video page](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k49a58878oo552/grand-theft-auto-v-official-gameplay-video.html) is dated July 2013. That predates PC Legacy; its embedded video did not render during this research, so no new timestamp-specific HUD or reaction observations are asserted here.

**Limits:** The inspected 2015 stills cannot establish normal third-person camera distance, collision behaviour, minimap details, detection logic or reaction timing. Steam’s legal credits name NaturalMotion euphoria motion synthesis technology. That establishes a credited technology, not which visible action uses it, its configuration, or Rockstar’s rig, topology, AI and animation graphs.

**Hide iT proposal:** Prototype one shoulder-follow camera that keeps the carried body, player feet and next corner visible. Check narrow doors with all three players present. Use a compact carry-state icon, interaction prompt and clearly distinct suspicion/alert signals; test whether the small map needs a minimap at all. For bloodless comedy, author a brief anticipation, exaggerated stumble and readable recovery, then add limited secondary limb motion. Keep carry attachments stable and bound physics impulses. These are affordable original experiments, not attempts to reproduce a proprietary simulation or an open-world population system.
