## Evidence rules

Use official/developer/store sources for claims, and inspect visible media when possible.
Separate direct visual observations, developer statements, and implementation inference.
Screenshots cannot establish exact source models, topology, rigging, shaders, budgets,
or asset provenance. Do not claim those details without published evidence.

A concise synthesis and original Blender asset study with role-distinct character
prototypes, a small modular environment/prop kit, and HUD/menu direction. Export
FBX files and validate scale/materials/hierarchy in the existing Unity project.
Keep the tested Carry Lab behavior intact; this is a separate asset/visual study,
not an implementation of all mission systems at once.
